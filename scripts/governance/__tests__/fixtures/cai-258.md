## OpenSpec

**Change ID:** `p1-11-tavily-work-prioritization`
**Depends on:** P1-10

## Problem

Regular caps save money only if the highest-value topics and articles are selected before work is claimed.

## Required behavior

Match and rank approved topics, queued RSS articles, and extracted-but-unanalyzed articles; reserve 30% capacity for oldest work; derive effective caps from remaining budget.

## Interfaces

Signal-to-topic matcher, deterministic signal-to-article matcher, priority score, 70/30 allocator, and budget-derived cap consumed by stage selection.

## Failure, security, and cost

Ambiguous or unmatched signals remain unlinked and never publish content. Old work retains guaranteed capacity. LLM ambiguity calls use reservations and a strict candidate cap.

## Out of scope

Topic approval.

## Given/When/Then test specification

### Unit

GIVEN exact URL, title/domain, topic alias, ambiguous, duplicate, and unmatched signals plus remaining budget, WHEN scoring and allocation run, THEN matches, effective cap, and 70/30 queues are deterministic.

### Integration

GIVEN persisted signals and eligible topic/article work, WHEN capped stage selection runs, THEN priority and age pools claim the specified proportions without duplicate claims.

### E2E

GIVEN trending and older non-trending fixture topics and articles under a low budget cap, WHEN repeated bounded slices run, THEN trending work runs first and older work is not starved.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Existing approved topics with strong signals are processed first.
- [ ] Tavily-matched articles are processed before lower-value recent articles within the priority pool.
- [ ] At least 30% of capacity remains available to oldest eligible work.
- [ ] The effective cap never exceeds the budget-derived affordable-unit estimate.
- [ ] Unmatched signals cannot directly publish content.
- [ ] OpenSpec artifacts are valid and synchronized with this issue.
- [ ] Unit, ordering integration, priority E2E, and regression tests pass.
- [ ] `/opsx:verify` and commit-bound `verify:pr-ready` pass before PR creation.
- [ ] Post-open CI and adversarial review pass before merge.

## Open questions

None.

Score weights: topic trend 40, exact Tavily article match 30, recency 15, backlog age 15. Allocation: 70% priority pool, 30% oldest-work pool, unused capacity flows to the other pool.
